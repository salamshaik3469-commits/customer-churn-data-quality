Data & interpretation note: This dashboard uses the cleaned, synthetic demonstration dataset in this project repository because the original Edvyro CSV was not supplied in the chat. Values are illustrative, not real company results. Churn rate = churned customers ÷ selected customers. Retention rate = retained customers ÷ selected customers. Monthly revenue at risk = sum of monthly charges for churned customers; it is an estimate of recurring charges, not a forecast or recognized revenue. Segment differences are descriptive and do not prove causation.
